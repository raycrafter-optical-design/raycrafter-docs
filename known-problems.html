Known problems

RayCrafter is pre-alpha. These are the known problems at the time of writing (v0.0.2); the current list is on GitHub under the known problem label.

Saving to Zemax

  • Saved ZMX files look wrong when opened in Zemax. Even a single achromat appears with very thick, elongated surfaces. Do not rely on exported files looking right in Zemax yet. #9
  • Fields do not survive a save and load. A ZMX file holds one aperture, one object distance, and a list of angles and wavelengths, so RayCrafter's per-field widths, vergences, offsets and colours are lost, and the fields that come back are not the ones saved. Loading shows a dialog listing the simplifications. #11
  • Plossl pairs come back as two separate lenses. The optics are right, but dragging one lens no longer moves the other. #12

Accuracy of the analysis

  • The MTF ignores a field's offset. Spot diagrams and ray fans include the offset; the MTF is calculated as if it were 0. #10
  • Side view draws some totally internally reflected rays wrongly. They are drawn running on to the lens edge rather than stopping. This affects the drawing only. #16

Internal issues

These are about the code rather than what you see, but they may affect speed or reliability.

  • Extra work while editing fields. Holding an arrow on a field-window spin box makes updates roughly twice as slow as needed. #13
  • MTF workaround for an Optiland error. The code that catches an Optiland error could hide other errors. #14
  • Unsaved-change tracking is in the interface, not the core model, so scripts cannot ask whether a design is saved. #15

Reporting a problem

Please open an issue with what you did, what you expected, and what happened.
